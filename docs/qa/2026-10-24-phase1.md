## Changes

Requester-only acknowledgments keep one action pending; unrelated snapshots cannot acknowledge it. Rejections return the latest viewer-specific state. Pending controls recover on rejection; bid selection remains when valid. Disconnection and a 15-second response watchdog resume the seat without replaying the uncertain action. A seat resumed elsewhere stops reconnecting. Create/join have pending feedback; join normalizes five English letters, supports Enter and retains inline failure feedback.

Focus follows stable control/card identities and phase/decision headings. Inspection focuses its enabled action (or Close when play is unavailable), then returns to the card on close. Decision revisions identify new steps; modal updates do not steal focus. Turn announcements are deduplicated. Bidding cards remain bright/inspectable, while play restrictions distinguish waiting, suit and Mary-forced selection.

## Limits and remaining checks

Fixtures exercise UI recovery; they do not certify real-network timing, physical swipes, installed PWA or screen-reader speech. Phase 1 does not complete the full 2/4/6/10-seat responsive matrix or phases 2–5. Initial connection stall, simultaneous rapid input, modal updates during a remote phase transition, and expired/removed-session UX were reviewed in code but not all reproduced in the browser. Existing room tests cover invalid reclaim, ten-seat capacity and reconnect grace. Match client/server deployment together because the pending-action protocol expects acknowledgments.
